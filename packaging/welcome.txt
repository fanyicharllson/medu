Welcome to Medu

Medu is a compiler for Medumba (Mə̀dʉ̂mbɑ̀), the language of Bangangté, Cameroon.

What Medu does
  - Translates Medumba sentences into English
  - Finds mistakes in sentences and explains how to fix them (with line and column)
  - Understands words typed without special letters (me nen zi = mə nɛ̌n zi)

Works offline
  Medu runs entirely on your computer. It needs no internet connection and sends no information anywhere.

What this installer will ask you
  1. To accept the licence (GPL-3.0, free and open source)
  2. Your name, so Medu can greet you (optional, stays on this computer)
  3. Where to install Medu
  4. A few options: add Medu to your terminal, open .mdt files with Medu, a desktop shortcut
